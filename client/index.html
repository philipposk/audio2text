Turn audio into text, then translate it

Translator writes out everything said in a recording. Upload a file, paste a YouTube, Vimeo or SoundCloud link, or use your microphone, then translate the transcript into more than 25 languages or turn it into subtitles.

What it does

  • Transcribes uploaded audio and video files
  • Works with YouTube, Vimeo and SoundCloud links
  • Live transcription from your microphone
  • Translates transcripts into more than 25 languages, or from another language into English
  • Makes subtitle files and can burn subtitles onto a video
  • Labels who said what when several people speak
  • Tidies up or rewrites the text with an AI helper
  • Exports to Word, PDF, plain text or subtitle files, and keeps a history of past transcripts
